# Pilot launch plan

1. Choose one geographic area and one equipment category to keep supply/demand concentration measurable.
2. Recruit 3–5 supplier companies for a concierge-assisted pilot; verify inventory, rates, service windows and delivery assumptions directly with owners.
3. Operate with payments disabled initially; handle agreements and payments outside the app only under a documented manual process approved by legal/accounting advisors. Do not represent manual payments as integrated.
4. Capture booking requests, fulfilment, cancellations, equipment downtime and repeat rate weekly.
5. Introduce pricing only after value and willingness-to-pay interviews.
6. Turn on marketplace PSP only after provider approval, business verification, country/currency compatibility, settlement/refund flow, webhook and reconciliation tests, legal terms and support procedures.

## Pilot success measures

Activation: supplier publishes a minimum threshold of listings with verified rate and availability. Value: real qualified rental requests and fulfilment rate. Retention: repeat use by supplier and renter. Reliability: booking conflict handling, cancellation/refund accuracy and support resolution. Record baseline and target before the pilot; do not invent performance data.
