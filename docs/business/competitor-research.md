# Competitor and adjacent-product scan

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-26. **Working geography:** Netherlands, as a launch hypothesis only; the owner must validate market and region before launch. This is a qualitative desk scan, not a market-size study or customer validation.

## Observed alternatives

| Product | Publicly observable position | Implication for our product |
|---|---|---|
| [Boels Rental](https://www.boels.com/en-nl/how-it-works) | Large direct rental operator with a broad equipment catalogue, deposit rules, delivery/collection and online rental requests. Its FAQ says a web request may be followed up by the next working day and some product prices are on request. | Do not compete on catalogue breadth alone. Potential wedge: cross-supplier discovery, shared availability and consistent comparison, especially across local independents. |
| [LOXAM Netherlands](https://www.loxam.nl/) | Direct rental provider with product categories, day/week/weekend price examples, location selection, and MyLoxam for quotes/order/invoice management. Some professional prices are account-personalized. | Customers already expect location, rental dates, rate breakdown and business account functions. A marketplace must make supplier-specific inclusions/exclusions explicit. |
| [mateco](https://mateco.nl/huren/) | Specialist professional access-equipment operator. Its rental assistant asks about working height/reach/capacity and sends a request for specialist follow-up; mymateco supports order management. | Vertical specialists know how to capture technical fit. Attribute-driven search must use safety-relevant typed specifications, not just text keywords. |
| [Entreeding](https://www.entreeding.com/) | Industry communication platform connecting Dutch/Belgian green, ground and infrastructure businesses; it describes machinery rental requests/offers, often with operator, and reports a network of 1,000+ members. | The B2B network/lead-generation concept is not novel. Differentiate with workflow reliability: timed availability, machine-level maintenance blocks, price snapshots and booking state management. Membership count is provider-stated, not independently audited. |
| [Renttoo](https://www.renttoo.nl/nl) | General rental marketplace for items near the renter, with tools & construction among categories. The public site uses “coming soon” language in some places. | Adjacent marketplace pattern, but current operational status and focus on professional construction equipment require verification before treating it as a direct competitor. |

## Evidence and limits

- Boels publishes product-level hire guidance, deposit differences, delivery/collection and rate-period distinctions ([official guide](https://www.boels.com/en-nl/how-it-works); [official FAQ](https://www.boels.com/nl-nl/customer-service/faq)).
- LOXAM describes personalized professional prices in MyLoxam and publicly lists rate periods on its Dutch homepage ([official site](https://www.loxam.nl/)).
- mateco's official flow is a rental request followed by specialist contact rather than a purely self-serve marketplace checkout ([official rental assistant](https://mateco.nl/huren/)).
- Entreeding and Renttoo are adjacent platforms but should not be assumed to have identical target segments or feature maturity.

No total addressable market, competitor revenue, or conversion metric is asserted here. Product pages can change; rerun this scan before any investment or launch decision.

## MVP differentiation hypothesis

1. Cross-supplier catalogue with comparable attributes, rates, deposit and location.
2. Server-authoritative date availability with a database-enforced no-overlap invariant.
3. Machine-level maintenance blocks that cannot collide with a live booking.
4. A supplier workspace that turns inquiries into trackable operational bookings.
5. Price snapshots and audit trail for business disputes.

These are hypotheses, not validated customer demand. The next step is 10–15 interviews with rental-company owners/operations managers plus renter-side project buyers; see `monetization.md` and `launch-plan.md`.
